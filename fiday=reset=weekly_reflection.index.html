Weekly Reflection

The week that was.

Fifteen minutes of honest reflection is worth more than a month of activity without awareness.

How to use this

This is a weekly practice, not a performance review. You are not looking for evidence that you did well or poorly. You are looking for patterns, signals, and the small shifts that accumulate into real change over time.

Complete it at the same time each week. The rhythm matters as much as the content. Download each session as a dated PDF and keep them. In three months, the pattern across all of them will tell you something no single session can.

"You cannot change what you do not see. And you cannot see clearly without stopping to look."

Five sections: how you are arriving, what happened this week, what moved beneath the surface, what you are taking from it, and where you are going next.

What the Week Taught You

Not what went wrong. What you learned. The distinction matters.

This section is not a place for self-criticism. You are not looking for evidence of failure. You are looking for information. Everything that happened this week, including the things that did not go as planned, contains data about how you work, what you need, and what is ready to shift.

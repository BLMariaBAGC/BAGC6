Which Needs Are Driving Your Income?

Week 3: Human Needs and Financial Behaviour

Introduction
Exercise 1 of 2

Your income is a strategy.

Your brain is not using it to pay your bills. It is using it to meet specific emotional needs. Until you understand which ones, the income ceiling stays where it is.

"Your income is not just a result. It is a strategy your brain uses to meet specific needs."

What this exercise reveals

Every financial pattern from the previous exercises has a need underneath it. The avoidance, the urgency, the comparison, the feeling of never enough. These are not character flaws. They are clever, if costly, strategies your brain developed to meet fundamental human needs.

This exercise identifies which two needs are most dominant in your financial life right now, traces the behaviours they are generating, and shows you exactly how those needs are keeping your income where it is.

"You are not stuck financially. You are loyal to a way of meeting your needs."

This exercise has four steps: identify your two dominant needs, link each need to the behaviours it creates, trace how those behaviours affect your income, and locate where the tension sits between over-met and under-met needs.

Step 1 of 4

Your Two Dominant Needs

Which two feel most alive in your financial life right now? Not the ones you aspire to. The ones that are actually driving your decisions.

Read each description in the context of money specifically, not life generally. The question is not which need you value most. The question is which two needs are most visibly shaping how you earn, spend, save, and respond to financial pressure.

🛡
Certainty
I feel safe when income is predictable
You need to know what is coming. Stability matters more than potential. Surprises feel threatening, even positive ones.
⚡
Uncertainty
I need excitement, risk, or movement
Routine feels suffocating. You are energised by new income ideas, opportunities, or ventures. Boredom is the real threat.
⭐
Significance
My income reflects my worth or status
What you earn is connected to how you feel about yourself. Being recognised, valued, or ahead of others matters in your financial decisions.
❤
Love and Connection
I stay aligned with people around me
Belonging shapes your financial behaviour. You may unconsciously limit growth to stay connected to family, peers, or the identity group you belong to.
🌿
Growth
I want to expand, learn, and increase capacity
Stagnation is intolerable. You invest in learning, expanding your skills, and pushing your capacity. Income often follows, but sometimes the investment outpaces the return.
🌐
Contribution
I want my work and money to matter
Purpose is the filter. You are drawn to work that has impact. Money without meaning feels hollow, which can create conflict between earning well and feeling good about it.

Step 2 of 4

Need to Behaviour

For each of your two dominant needs, complete the sentence. Be as specific and honest as you can. What does this need actually make you do?

The behaviour does not have to look dramatic. "I avoid looking at my figures when income dips" is as valid as "I overwork to prove my value." The point is to see the mechanism clearly.

Step 3 of 4

The Income Link

This is the breakthrough step. How does the strategy your brain is running to meet this need keep your income where it is?

Your income level is not an accident and not a talent problem. It is the current output of the strategy your dominant needs are running. Complete both sentences as specifically as you can.

This keeps my income at...
Describe the pattern or ceiling. Not a number necessarily, but a dynamic.
Because it allows me to feel...
This is the payoff the strategy is protecting. Name the feeling.
"My income is not the problem. It is the current solution."
Step 4 of 4

The Tension

Every income plateau has a tension beneath it. One need is being over-served. Another is being starved. Naming this is what opens the space for change.

Over-met does not mean this need is wrong. It means the strategy for meeting it has become so dominant that it is limiting other things. Under-met means this need is present but the current financial strategy is not serving it.

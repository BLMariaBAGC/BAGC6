Step 3 of 3

Your Emotional Response

The numbers you have just produced will trigger a response. That response is information. Not a verdict.

When a figure feels unrealistic or uncomfortable, this is often not an accurate assessment of what is achievable. It is the nervous system flagging unfamiliarity. The brain marks unfamiliar as unsafe. That is not reality. That is conditioning. Notice the response, name it, and choose your interpretation.

The freedom it would create
The experiences it would allow
What it would mean for my family
The security it would bring
Being able to invest in myself properly
The options it would open

"The discomfort you felt in this exercise is not a signal to reduce the target. It is a signal that your nervous system has not yet caught up with what is possible. That gap is exactly what the programme is designed to close."

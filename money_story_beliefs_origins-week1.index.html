Part Three: Beliefs and Origins

You have identified your beliefs and seen what they cost you. Now you go one layer deeper. Every belief you are carrying was taught to you. Not by your character, not by reality, but by a specific set of circumstances that no longer exist.

Part Three · Step 3 of 5

Where Did This Come From?

This belief was not born with you. It was formed in a specific context. Answer each question as precisely as you can. You are not looking for a tidy story. You are looking for the moment this became the way things are.

How old were you when you first held this belief?
An approximate age is fine. Let the feeling guide you to the right era, not the memory.
Who or what shaped this belief?
A person, a household pattern, a community, a religious environment, a financial event. It may be more than one source.
What were the specific circumstances?
What was actually happening at the time that made this belief feel true, or necessary?
Was this belief protective at the time?
Almost every limiting belief was once adaptive. It helped you belong, stay safe, avoid conflict, or make sense of a confusing environment. Naming this removes the shame.

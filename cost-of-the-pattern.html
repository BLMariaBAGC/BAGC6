Step 1 of 4

Which pattern created the strongest response when you read it in the workshop?

The one that tightened something. The one you recognised before you finished the sentence.

Pattern 1 · Self-Worth
"You have the evidence. You still feel like it is only a matter of time before someone notices."
You over-prepare and hold back the ask, and your success still feels provisional. This is not a confidence gap. It is a belief that you are not quite enough.
Guilt · Shame · Unworthiness
Pattern 2 · Control and Responsibility
"If you let go of it, it falls apart. So you carry all of it."
You find it hard to delegate, hard to rest, hard to trust it will hold without you. You pay for it in your energy, your health, and the growth you will not allow.
Pressure · Overwhelm · Isolation
Pattern 3 · Belonging and Acceptance
"You are in the room, but part of you is still waiting to be found out."
You keep your voice measured and your real self managed, because being fully seen feels like a risk. The ceiling is not skill. It is the fear that being seen ends in rejection.
Fear of rejection · Identity conflict · Belonging
Pattern 4 · Safety and Vulnerability
"Visibility feels dangerous. So you stay smaller than you are."
You hold back from the bigger role or the public moment, even when you could do it. Not because you cannot, but because exposure feels unsafe below the level of thought.
Vigilance · Avoidance · Self-protection

Step 2 of 4

Where is this pattern costing you?

A self-doubt pattern runs through every area of your life. Select every area where you can feel its impact. Choose as many as are true.

Regulation
Health and Nervous System
The pattern has become a background setting. The tension, the second-guessing, the bracing for judgement. It affects your sleep, your body, your ability to rest and recover. You carry it physically.
Direction
Vision and Goals
There are moves you have not made and a version of your future you have not fully committed to, because the pattern kept the door half closed before you reached it.
Connection
Relationships
The pattern shows up in at least one relationship that matters to you, as tension, avoidance, or a version of yourself that stays managed. The conversation you have not had is affecting the connection you have.
Communication
How You Speak About Your Worth
In the moments that mattered, about your value, your terms, your contribution, you have said less than you know, agreed to less than you deserve, or left before the real thing was named.
Identity
Who You Believe You Are
Your sense of who you are, professionally and personally, is shaped by what the pattern has told you that you are allowed to want, reach for, or become. The ceiling is not ability. It is permission.
Prosperity
Direct Financial Cost
Your current earnings do not fully reflect what your experience, skill, and contribution warrant. There is a gap between what you produce and what the pattern allows you to receive.

Candle Confirmation
Engulfing Candles
Bullish Engulfing
Green candle body fully covers the prior red candle. Bullish reversal signal at support.
Bearish Engulfing
Red candle body fully covers the prior green candle. Bearish reversal signal at resistance.
Wait for the engulfing candle to close, then wait for the NEXT candle to close in the same direction before entering. That second candle confirms the move is real, not a fake-out.
Three Soldiers & Three Crows
Three Soldiers
Three consecutive rising green candles. Strong upward momentum. Wait for all three to close.
Three Crows
Three consecutive falling red candles. Strong downward momentum. Wait for all three to close.
Each candle should open within or near the body of the previous candle and close beyond it. Wait for all three to fully close before entering.
Pullback vs Reversal
Pullback (continue)
Temporary counter-trend move. Trend resumes. Wait for it to close before deciding.
Reversal (change)
Price makes lower highs and lower lows. Trend has changed. Avoid prior-trend entries.
If price is below EMA9 and below VWAP and you get a green candle, that is a pullback, not a reversal. Wait for that candle to close. If the next candle continues going up, it may be reversing. If it goes back down, it was just a pullback. You can have two or three opposite candles in a pullback before it resumes the original direction. Do not enter on a pullback candle.

Trading Framework ▼
Russell and Isabella's 5-step framework for reading price action before entering a trade.
Step 1
Establish the Higher Timeframe Trend
Check the Daily and 4-hour charts first. Identify whether price is making higher highs and higher lows (uptrend), lower highs and lower lows (downtrend), or ranging sideways. You only take trades in the direction of this higher timeframe trend. Trading against it dramatically reduces your probability of success.
Step 2
Identify a Key Level
Mark horizontal support and resistance zones where price has previously reversed or stalled. These are the locations where institutional orders tend to cluster. A level is more significant if price has reacted to it multiple times across different timeframes. Only consider setups that form at or very near these key levels.
Step 3
Wait for a Candle Pattern Signal
Once price reaches the key level, wait for a candlestick pattern that confirms rejection or continuation before entering. Do not anticipate -- let the candle close before deciding.
Candle Pattern Reference
Engulfing Candles
Bullish Engulfing
Green candle body fully covers the prior red candle. Bullish reversal signal at support.
Bearish Engulfing
Red candle body fully covers the prior green candle. Bearish reversal signal at resistance.
The engulfing candle must close beyond the entire body (not just wick) of the previous candle. The larger the engulfing candle relative to the first, the stronger the signal. Always confirm at a key level.
Three Soldiers & Three Crows
Three Soldiers
Three consecutive rising green candles, each closing higher. Strong bullish momentum signal.
Three Crows
Three consecutive falling red candles, each closing lower. Strong bearish momentum signal.
Each candle should open within or near the body of the previous candle and close beyond it. Small or absent wicks strengthen the signal. Context matters: Three Soldiers after a prolonged downtrend is more significant than mid-trend.
Pullback vs Reversal
Pullback (continue)
Temporary counter-trend move. Trend resumes. Use for entry in trend direction.
Reversal (change)
Price makes lower highs and lower lows. Trend direction has changed. Avoid prior-trend entries.
A pullback stays above (in an uptrend) the prior swing low and resumes without breaking structure. A reversal breaks structure: it creates a new lower low (uptrend) or higher high (downtrend). Volume expanding on the break confirms a reversal. A pullback on low volume against the trend is a buying opportunity; the same move on heavy volume is a warning.
Step 4
Define Your Risk Before Entry
Before placing the trade, know exactly where your stop loss goes and how many points that represents. Calculate your position size from the Risk Per Trade Calculator so you never risk more than your set percentage per trade. If the stop placement means an unfavourable risk/reward ratio (less than 1:1.5), skip the trade.
Step 5
Execute and Manage
Enter when the signal candle closes. Set your stop and take profit immediately -- do not move your stop loss against the trade. Once price moves 50% toward your target, consider moving stop to breakeven. Log the trade in the Paper Trading Log above so you build a record of your decision-making over time.

Introduction
Release a Belief

One belief.
One session. One release.

You do not need to clear everything at once. Each belief you work on this way loosens the one next to it.

How this works

This exercise is for beliefs you have already identified through any of the money story exercises or through your own awareness. You bring one here. You trace whether it still holds real authority over your life today. You speak to the memory that created it. And then you cut the tie.

You can return to this exercise as many times as you need. Each session works on one belief. One belief held with full attention is worth more than ten beliefs processed quickly.

"The belief is not yours. It was borrowed from a moment that is over. You get to give it back."

Five steps: name the belief, check whether it still holds true, speak to the memory that created it, cut the cord, install the replacement. A private PDF downloads at the end.

Step 1 of 5

Name the Belief

Write it exactly as it lives in your head. Not a polished version. The actual words your inner voice uses.

This may be a belief from your money story exercises or one that has surfaced more recently. Write it in the first person, present tense. "I am not the kind of person who..." or "Money always..." Whatever form it takes for you.

The belief I am working with today

Take a moment before writing. Let the one with the most charge surface, not the most comfortable.

The belief is: ...
Step 2 of 5

Is It Still True?

This belief was formed in a specific context that may no longer exist. Answer honestly, not charitably.

The question is not whether this belief was ever understandable. The question is whether it is factually true about you right now, in your actual current life. Answer as the adult you are today.

The circumstances that created this belief still exist in my life today.
The person or environment that taught me this belief still has authority over my financial decisions.
When I look at the evidence of my actual adult life, this belief accurately describes what is true for me.
This belief still serves me in a meaningful way. It protects something real or prevents genuine harm.

Step 3 of 5

Speak to the Memory

Before you release the belief, you acknowledge where it came from. This is the step that makes the release feel true rather than forced.

Every limiting belief was once a form of protection, belonging, or sense-making. The younger version of you who developed it was not wrong. Acknowledging this removes the shame and resistance that would otherwise follow you into the release.

To the memory that created this belief

Speak directly to it. Acknowledge what it gave you. Tell it clearly that it is no longer needed.

I understand why I learned this. At the time, it helped me by...
What did this belief protect you from? What did it help you avoid, belong to, or survive?
And I want to say: thank you. You did your job. But the world you were protecting me from no longer exists, and...
What has changed? What can you see now that you could not see then? What do you have now that you did not have when this belief formed?
So I am choosing to release you now. You are not needed here anymore.
Write anything else you need to say to complete this acknowledgement. Nothing is too small or too personal.
Step 4 of 5

Cut the Cord

You have named it, checked its authority, and spoken to it. Now you consciously sever the connection.

Take a breath. Read the cord below. Feel the weight of the connection. When you are fully present with what you are releasing, cut the tie.

The Tie Between Then and Now

The cord carries the belief from the moment it formed to who you are today

Then
The moment it formed
You, today
Present

The cord is cut. The tie no longer holds.
Write what is true from the other side of this.
Step 5 of 5

Install the New Belief

The space left by the old belief is not empty. Fill it deliberately. Not with aspiration, with something already in the process of being true.

The replacement should feel slightly uncomfortable: not impossible, but stretching. If it feels completely natural it has not shifted far enough. Find the version that is true and bigger than what you have allowed yourself to hold until now.

Specific. Present tense. Grounded in evidence that already exists.

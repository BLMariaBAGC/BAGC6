Your Dominant Pattern

Based on your responses, the pattern creating the most interference is:

This result reflects the pattern with the highest score across your responses. Most professionals carry elements of more than one pattern. This identifies where the most interference is currently occurring.

Self-Worth

What becomes possible when self-worth is no longer the question.

What this pattern has been costing

A systematic review of 62 studies found that professionals carrying this pattern experience consistently lower job satisfaction, reduced advancement rates, and higher burnout. A 2024 survey of 10,000 professionals found 71% of CEOs carry it. In the UK, 62% of working adults report the same. The pattern is common. The cost is not small.

What shifts when the pattern does

You negotiate your rate or salary without the familiar contraction in your chest.
You step into visibility without the preparation overload that currently costs you hours.
You receive recognition and allow it to settle, rather than immediately finding reasons to discount it.
You move toward the next level without waiting to feel ready first.
The evidence of your competence becomes something your brain actually uses.

Research foundation: Bravata et al. (2020) systematic review, Journal of General Internal Medicine. Korn Ferry Workforce 2024 Global Insights Report. UK national study, Funding Guru (2025).

Control and Responsibility

What becomes possible when you stop carrying it all.

What this pattern has been costing

Mental Health UK's 2026 Burnout Report found that 91% of UK adults report high or extreme stress levels, with one in five needing time off due to mental health. For professionals whose pattern says everything depends on them, the cost accumulates in strategic capacity, physical health, and the ability to lead rather than manage.

What shifts when the pattern does

You delegate without the background hum of anxiety about whether it will be done properly.
You spend your energy on the work only you can do.
You lead from authority rather than from the exhausting need to prove nothing will fail without you.
Your health stops paying the price the current pattern extracts.
You build a team that performs, because you let it.

Research foundation: Mental Health UK Burnout Report (2026). Research consistently links perfectionism-driven control patterns to burnout, reduced strategic output, and impaired team performance.

Belonging and Acceptance

What becomes possible when you stop managing the room.

What this pattern has been costing

Google's Project Aristotle found that the felt sense of belonging is the single strongest predictor of team performance, more than collective IQ, technical skills, or experience. When professionals spend energy managing how they are perceived rather than contributing what they know, the cost shows in their output, their influence, and the relationships they build.

What shifts when the pattern does

You speak with the same confidence in a room of fifty as in a room of five.
You hold your position under challenge without the pull to disappear or over-explain.
You contribute ideas without the pre-filter of whether they will land well.
You stop modifying yourself for different audiences and let the consistency do the work.
Your influence grows, because people feel who you actually are.

Research foundation: Google Project Aristotle. Edmondson, A.C. (1999). Psychological safety and learning behaviour in work teams. Administrative Science Quarterly, 44(2), 350-383.

Safety and Vulnerability

What becomes possible when visibility stops feeling like a threat.

What this pattern has been costing

Research found that approximately 47% of professionals say fear of failure holds them back at work, rising to 60 to 70% among highly educated professionals. Each avoided opportunity reinforces the neural pathway that says exposure is dangerous. The career you have built reflects your managed risk tolerance, not your actual capability.

What shifts when the pattern does

You pursue the role that currently sits just out of reach.
You say yes to high-visibility opportunities before the self-doubt has time to respond.
You back your own ideas in rooms where the decisions are made.
You build a body of work that reflects your actual capability rather than your caution.
The life you have been keeping yourself from starts to feel accessible.

Research foundation: Brassey, J. and van Witteloostuijn, A. research on fear of failure and career progression, via Workplace Insight (2019). Study of 1,000+ professionals.
